# Ship 30 for 30 Writing Skill & Framework

## 1. Core Philosophy
The Ship 30 for 30 framework is built on reader-first digital writing:
- Zero throat-clearing or academic preamble.
- High visual rhythm and scannability.
- Actionable, tactical frameworks rather than vague platitudes.
- Narrative progression that carries the reader from tension to transformation.

## 2. Structural Requirements
Every generated essay must follow this 5-part architecture:
1. **The Irresistible Hook (75–100 words):**
   - Single-sentence opening tension that challenges conventional wisdom.
   - States what is at stake and promises a concrete outcome.
2. **The Shift / Problem Diagnosis (200–250 words):**
   - Breaks down why traditional approaches fail.
   - Introduces the paradigm shift uncovered in Lenny's conversations.
3. **Core Pillar 1: Strategic Foundations (250–300 words):**
   - First concrete pillar with a named framework.
   - Grounded in guest quotes and transcript citations `[Episode title — Guest]`.
4. **Core Pillar 2: Tactical Execution & Nuance (250–300 words):**
   - Specific step-by-step playbook or operating rhythm.
   - Contrasting examples from high-growth tech companies.
5. **Core Pillar 3: Common Pitfalls & Counter-Intuitive Truths (200–250 words):**
   - What top 1% practitioners avoid that amateurs fall into.
6. **The Takeaway Checklist & Final Provocation (100–150 words):**
   - Summary bulleted checklist ready for immediate implementation on Monday morning.

## 3. Formatting & Visual Cadence Rules
- **Short Paragraphs:** 1 to 3 sentences maximum. White space is breathing room.
- **Hierarchical Headings:** Clear H2 (`##`) and H3 (`###`) anchors for visual navigation.
- **Bulleted Lists:** Break complex breakdowns into digestible bullet points.
- **Selective Bold:** Bold the first 2–4 words of key lines or pivotal frameworks. Never bold whole paragraphs.
- **Target Length:** ~1,250 words (acceptable bounds: 1,125 – 1,375 words, ±10%).

## 4. Grounding & Citation Mandate
- No claim may be fabricated from general internet lore.
- Every tactical principle must trace back to the retrieved transcript chunks.
- Format all citations inline as: `[Episode Title — Guest Name]`.
